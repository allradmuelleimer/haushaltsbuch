Budget app for Android

A household budget, radically simple.

Adding an expense means: type the amount, tap Save. One large number shows how much is left this month after your fixed costs. No account, no bank connection, no cloud.

Get it on Google Play

Record

An expense in two taps

Type the amount, tap Save – nothing else is required. Category, description, date and note are optional, and you can add them to the entry later.

Right after saving, Undo appears in case you mistyped. Forgotten expenses can be added later in the history, with date and time.

Receipts

Hold it up to the camera instead of typing

Tap the camera icon and hold the receipt in view. The app reads the total, the shop, date and time and fills them in.

Text recognition runs on your phone. The image is neither stored nor sent anywhere.

Fixed costs

Enter them once, then forget them

Rent, electricity, insurance, subscriptions: during setup the app asks for one item at a time – leave it empty if you don't have it. Yearly costs such as liability insurance get a due date.

From then on the app deducts your fixed costs every month by itself. The large number on the home screen is what you really have left.

Analysis

The whole month in three numbers

Spent, left, average per day. Plus a chart by day and the breakdown of your month: income, fixed costs, to spend.

No dashboards, no twelve charts. What's left can roll into the next month if you want.

Your numbers stay on your phone

Everything you enter lives only on your device. There is no server your finances could end up on.

  • No account, no sign-in – open the app and start.
  • No bank connection – you never enter login details anywhere.
  • No cloud, no usage statistics, no crash reports. That also means no sync between devices.
  • Ads from day seven: one banner at the top of the home screen. The ads never see a single number from your budget. A one-time purchase removes them for good – there is no subscription.
  • Seven languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese. The currency follows your region.

Questions

What does the app cost?

You can use it with ads. From the seventh day it shows one banner at the top of the home screen. The one-time purchase “Remove ads” switches it off for good; there is no subscription.

Do I need an account or my bank details?

No. There is no sign-in and no bank connection. You enter income, fixed costs and expenses yourself, and everything stays on your phone.

Do I have to pick a category for every expense?

No. Only the amount is required. Category, description and note are optional. When you type a description, the app suggests a matching category – learned from your own entries, on the device only, and it can be switched off.

Can I use the app on two phones?

No. Because there is no cloud and no server, the app doesn't sync between devices. That's the price of your numbers never leaving your phone.

What happens to money left over at the end of the month?

You decide in the settings: “Carry surplus over” moves what's left into the next month, “Carry shortfall over” deducts a deficit next month. Each can be switched on or off. More: Money left at the end of the month.

When will the app be available?

The app is in its final closed test and will be on Google Play for Android shortly.
