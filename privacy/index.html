Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · The German version is authoritative.

Summary

Your finances never leave your device. Income, fixed costs, expenses, categories and settings are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of our own.

Three Google services are built in: ads (Google AdMob, until you remove them), purchases (Google Play) and the request for a rating (Google Play). What each of them receives is described below. The ads never see a single number from your budget.

What the app stores on your device

  • your income and fixed costs with label, amount and schedule
  • your entries (expenses and one-off income) with amount, date, the time you entered them and — if given — label, category and note
  • your settings: budget start day, carry-overs, your list of categories
  • an unsaved draft, so nothing is lost when you switch apps; it is cleared if left untouched for more than three hours
  • a cached purchase status (whether ads are removed), so no ad appears while offline, and whether the app has already asked for a rating

The app suggests categories and labels based on the weekday, time and amount of your past entries. This analysis happens on the device only. It deliberately does not learn from sensitive entries, and suggestions can be switched off in the settings.

Scanning receipts

When you tap the camera icon, the app opens the camera and reads the receipt straight from the live image: total, shop, date and time. For this it asks once for the camera permission. Alternatively you can pick a photo from your gallery; the app then receives only that one image.

Text recognition (Google ML Kit, provided through Google Play services) runs entirely on the device. Images and recognised text are neither stored nor transferred – only the total, shop, date and time go into your entry, and only once you save. Google Play services may send technical diagnostic data about how text recognition performs (such as device model, errors, performance figures) to Google, but no images and no text from the receipt.

None of this is transferred to us. We run no server and there is no user account. Apart from the camera for receipts, the app does not use the microphone, location, contacts, bank accounts or your files, and it sends no crash reports or usage statistics.

Advertising

From the seventh day after installation, the app shows a banner at the top of its start screen, served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

Depending on your consent, Google processes your device's advertising ID, technical device details (such as model, operating system, language), your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, and usage data about the ads (e.g. impressions and clicks). The ads library also sends technical diagnostic data about its own operation to Google. The purposes are to select and deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and — if you agree — personalise ads. Google processes some of this data as an independent controller and may share it with advertising partners.

The ads never see your finances. Amounts, entries, categories, labels or budget figures are never passed to the ads library.

Consent: where required by law — in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — a Google dialog (User Messaging Platform) asks you before the first ad whether and how your data may be used for advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings → Ads → “Privacy for ads”. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

Removing ads: after the one-time purchase, the app loads no ads and shows no consent dialog.

Purchases via Google Play

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play (Google Play Billing). You enter payment details with Google only; the app never sees them. It only learns from Google Play whether the purchase exists and acknowledges it as required.

Request for a rating

About one and two months after installation, the app asks for a rating using Google Play's built-in in-app review. The card comes from Google, and Google decides whether it appears. If you rate, the rating goes to Google Play under your Google account. The app does not learn whether or how you rated.

Permissions

The app itself requests only the camera (to read receipts when you tap the camera icon; images are neither stored nor transferred). The following are added by Google's libraries: internet and network state (only for ads, purchases and the rating request), advertising ID and Android ad services, Google Play Billing, and foreground service / wake lock (short background tasks of the ads library).

Legal bases

  • App features: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. This data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG; where no consent is required, non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in funding the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Purchases: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Rating request and this website: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Transfers outside the EU

Google may process data from ads, purchases and ratings outside the EU, in particular in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and additionally uses the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention and backup

Data on the device remains until you delete it in the app, uninstall the app or clear its storage. The app does not take part in Android's automatic backup to your Google account, so your finances do not end up in a cloud this way either — but they are gone after uninstalling or switching devices. Google keeps ad, purchase and rating data according to its own retention periods.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21) and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). Since we hold no data about you, you exercise most of these directly on your device. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Data Protection Commissioner of Lower Saxony.

Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no third-party fonts or scripts and runs no analytics. When you visit it, the host GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) processes technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested file) to deliver the site and fend off attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
